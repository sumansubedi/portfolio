---
title: The edge case is the point
date: 2026-01-12
summary: Anyone can check the happy path. The job is everything that happens at the edges.
---

The happy path is a courtesy, not a test. It tells you the feature can work, not that it
does work.

What I actually look for lives at the boundaries: the empty state, the slow network, the
double-click, the input nobody thought a person would type. The interesting failures are
almost never in the middle of the range — they're at zero, at the maximum, at the moment
two things happen at once.

Good QA isn't pessimism. It's curiosity about where the seams are, and the patience to
press on each one until it either holds or tells you something useful.
